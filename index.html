Our product

Accountability tools for addiction recovery programs.

ClearPath Monitoring is a patient-monitoring platform for substance use disorder treatment facilities and sober living homes. A secure staff dashboard and companion apps on a patient's own devices give care teams location awareness, app and web safeguards, tamper alerts and direct messaging — designed from the start around patient privacy.
