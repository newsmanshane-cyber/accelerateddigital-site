In short: We only text people who have agreed to it. Reply STOP to any message to stop, START to rejoin and HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply. We never share your number for marketing.

1. Who Sends These Messages

Text messages described on this page are sent by Accelerated Digital Solutions LLC ("ADS"), Los Angeles, California. ADS is also the developer and operator of ClearPath Monitoring, so ClearPath beta program texts are sent by ADS as well.

2. Our Messaging Programs

  • ADS client messages: Appointment confirmations and reminders, service and project updates, and replies to questions you've asked us. No marketing or promotional messages.
  • ClearPath beta program texts: Notices of new ClearPath beta builds, testing instructions, and requests for feedback or short surveys. No marketing or promotional messages, and never anything about health, treatment or other personal information.

The ClearPath beta program also has its own SMS terms at clearpathmonitoring.com/sms-terms. They say the same thing. If anything ever differs, the ClearPath page governs ClearPath beta texts.

3. How You Opt In

  • ADS client messages: You give us your mobile number and agree to receive texts from us, for example on a service agreement, by email, or by texting us first. Agreeing to texts is never required to do business with us.
  • ClearPath beta program texts: You tick the optional box "Text me updates about the ClearPath beta program" on the sign-up form at clearpathmonitoring.com/beta and submit it with your mobile number. The box is never pre-ticked, and you can join the beta without it. After you sign up, we send one text confirming your subscription.

4. Frequency & Costs

  • ADS client messages: Message frequency varies with your project or appointments.
  • ClearPath beta program texts: Up to 4 messages per month, sent only between 8am and 9pm Pacific time.
  • Costs: Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. We do not charge for these messages.

5. Opting Out & Help

  • Stop: Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You'll receive one final message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no further messages after that.
  • Rejoin: Reply START to subscribe again.
  • Help: Reply HELP to any message, or email info@accelerateddigital.net (ADS client messages) or support@clearpathmonitoring.com (ClearPath beta texts).

6. Your Privacy

We use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to and to contact you about the related service or program.

  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
  • We keep a record of your consent (the date, time and wording you agreed to) and of the messages we send and receive, so we can show what you agreed to and honor an opt-out.
  • Messages are delivered by Twilio on our behalf.

See our Privacy Policy for more. ClearPath beta testers should also see the ClearPath Privacy Policy.

7. Carriers

Supported on all major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update these terms. If we change what we send or how often, we'll ask for your consent again.
